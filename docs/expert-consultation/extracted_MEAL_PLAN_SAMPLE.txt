Nutrition and Cost Notes

PhilFCT/DOST-FNRI is acknowledged as the preferred local reference for future ingredient-level verification and correction. Full PhilFCT-based nutrient mapping is listed as a future research and system enhancement recommendation.

Values marked as api_estimate are draft ingredient-sum estimates, not final clinical values. They require professional validation or correction against stronger references such as PhilFCT.
Ingredient lists may show the original batch recipe. The planned meal slot represents one serving/person. The cost table shows batch/catalog cost separately from cost per planned serving.

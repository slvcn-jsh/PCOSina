PCOSINA is an offline-first, Filipino-focused meal planning app for individuals with PCOS. It is a decision-support tool for wellness guidance, not a medical treatment system. Our problem is that existing nutrition apps are generic and lack PCOS-specific, culturally relevant, pantry-aware, and offline-capable planning for Filipinos. The project uses a two-stage algorithm: a rule-based filtering step that applies user preferences, restrictions, pantry items, and budget, followed by MILP optimization that selects a 7-day plan meeting nutrition, cost, and variety constraints. This approach is explainable and constraint-aware, which is why we chose MILP over machine learning. The architecture includes the Android client, local storage, the optimization engine, and a recipe database. Offline-first means plans, groceries, and progress logs are stored locally; generating a new plan still requires backend connectivity. Evaluation follows ISO/IEC 25010 with alpha and beta testing, descriptive statistics, Cronbach's alpha for reliability, and computation precision checks. In summary, we built a practical, culturally localized meal planning system that aligns with Philippine dietary guidance and real-world constraints, and is ready for broader evaluation and dataset expansion.
